# Grand Tour: candidate reading and viewing

A first, deliberately generous list to choose from. Split into **books**,
**internet sources** and **other sources** (films, documentaries, podcasts,
places), and within each by topic.

- ★ marks a source with **place-specific passages** worth extracting for the
  app's Reading tab.
- Language: EN, FR, NL, IT, DE (original language; most classics also exist in
  translation).
- Topics: Grand Tour · Gastronomy · Wine · Architecture · Museums & art ·
  Riviera · Road · Luxembourg · Basel · Modena · Liguria · Menton · Grasse ·
  Avignon & Provence · December.

## 1. Books

### The historical Grand Tour
- ★ **Michel de Montaigne, *Journal de voyage en Italie*** (1580–81, published 1774). FR. Montaigne rode through Basel on his way to Italy and wrote about the city, its baths, food and Protestant debates. A Grand Tour before the term existed. *Basel*
- ★ **Tobias Smollett, *Travels through France and Italy*** (1766). EN. A famously grumpy traveller who wintered in Nice; the Riviera, its coast road and the inns of the day. *Riviera*
- **Laurence Sterne, *A Sentimental Journey through France and Italy*** (1768). EN. Sterne's answer to Smollett, whom he mocks as "Smelfungus": the gentle, feeling traveller.
- **Johann Wolfgang von Goethe, *Italienische Reise*** (1816–17). DE. The founding text of the German love affair with Italy. Not our route, but the spirit of the journey.
- ★ **Stendhal, *Rome, Naples et Florence*** (1817/1826). FR. Travel impressions along the Via Emilia, including Modena and its duchy. *Modena*
- ★ **Stendhal, *Mémoires d'un touriste*** (1838). FR. A journey through provincial France, including Avignon and the Rhône valley. *Avignon*
- ★ **Henry James, *A Little Tour in France*** (1884). EN. Elegant chapters on Avignon, Orange, Nîmes, the Pont du Gard and Arles. *Avignon & Provence*
- **Christopher Hibbert, *The Grand Tour*** (1969). EN. The readable classic history.
- **Jeremy Black, *The British Abroad: The Grand Tour in the Eighteenth Century*** (1992). EN. The scholarly standard; also his *Italy and the Grand Tour* (2003).
- **Rosemary Sweet, *Cities and the Grand Tour: The British in Italy, c.1690–1820*** (2012). EN. How Grand Tourists saw individual cities.
- **Ilja Leonard Pfeijffer, *Grand Hotel Europa*** (2018). NL. A novel about Europe, its past and its tourists: the Grand Tour turned inside out.
- **Geert Mak, *In Europa*** (2004). NL. A year's journey through twentieth-century Europe; the background to almost everywhere we pass.

### Gastronomy
- **Massimo Bottura, *Never Trust a Skinny Italian Chef*** (Phaidon, 2014). EN. Fifty dishes and the stories behind Osteria Francescana. *Modena*
- **Massimo Bottura, *Bread Is Gold*** (Phaidon, 2017). EN. The Refettorio project: great chefs cooking with surplus food.
- **Mauro Colagreco, *Mirazur*** (2018). EN/FR. The restaurant, its gardens and the Riviera in his cooking. *Menton*
- **Mauro Colagreco & Laura Colagreco, *Under the Sign of the Moon*** (Abrams, 2024). EN. Mirazur's biodynamic gardens and lunar menus. *Menton*
- **Massimo Montanari, *Il cibo come cultura*** (2004) and ***Italian Identity in the Kitchen*** (2013). IT/EN. The Bologna food historian on why Emilian food tastes of history. *Modena*
- ★ **Elizabeth David, *Italian Food*** (1954) and ***French Provincial Cooking*** (1960). EN. Still the best prose about both cuisines; strong regional chapters on Emilia and Provence.
- **Waverley Root, *The Food of France*** (1958) and ***The Food of Italy*** (1971). EN. Region-by-region cultural histories of cooking.
- ★ **M. F. K. Fisher, *Two Towns in Provence*** (1964). EN. Aix and Marseille, by the great American food writer. *Provence*
- **Peter Mayle, *A Year in Provence*** (1989). EN. Light, but the Luberon near Avignon in its first and best telling. *Avignon & Provence*

### Wine
- ★ **Kermit Lynch, *Adventures on the Wine Route*** (1988). EN. A wine importer's travels through French cellars, including Châteauneuf-du-Pape and the southern Rhône. *Avignon*

### Architecture
- **Nikolaus Pevsner, *An Outline of European Architecture*** (1943). EN. The classic overview, from Romanesque (Modena) to modern (Vitra).
- **Arthur Kingsley Porter, *Lombard Architecture*** (1915–17). EN. The pioneering study of north Italian Romanesque, including Modena's cathedral. For the specialist. *Modena*
- **Caroline Constant, *Eileen Gray*** (Phaidon, 2000). EN. Including E-1027 at Roquebrune-Cap-Martin. *Menton*
- **Le Corbusier and the Cabanon**: several monographs; easier through Cap Moderne's own material (see §2). *Menton*

### Museums & art
- ★ **Fyodor Dostoevsky, *The Idiot*** (1869) and **Anna Dostoevskaya's diary**. RU/EN/FR/NL. In 1867 Dostoevsky stood transfixed before Holbein's *Dead Christ in the Tomb* in Basel; the painting returns in the novel. One of literature's great encounters with a single painting. *Basel*
- **Jacob Burckhardt, *Die Kultur der Renaissance in Italien*** (1860). DE. Written by a Basel professor about Italy: a bridge between two chapters of our trip. *Basel*
- **Erasmus, *Moriae Encomium / Lof der Zotheid*** (1511). LA/NL/EN/FR. Read with Holbein's marginal drawings in mind: the copy he illustrated is in the Kunstmuseum Basel. *Basel*
- **Colin B. Bailey, Alan Hollinghurst & Xavier Salomon, *Fragonard's Progress of Love*** (Frick Diptych, 2021). EN. The panels Fragonard brought to his cousin's villa in Grasse. *Grasse*
- **Jean Cocteau**: his drawings for Menton's wedding room are best seen in person; for reading, *Journal d'un inconnu* (1953). FR. *Menton*

### Luxembourg and Basel
- ★ **Victor Hugo**, letters and notes from his stays in Vianden (1862–71), collected for the Maison de Victor Hugo there. FR. *Luxembourg*
- ★ **Theodor Herzl, diaries** (1895–1904). DE/EN. The First Zionist Congress, Basel 1897. *Basel*
- **Friedrich Nietzsche**, letters from his Basel years (1869–79). DE. *Basel*

### Modena and Emilia
- **Michèle K. Spike, *Tuscan Countess: The Life and Extraordinary Times of Matilda of Canossa*** (2004). EN. The countess whose lands framed Romanesque Modena. *Modena*
- **Stendhal, *La Chartreuse de Parme*** (1839). FR. Set in a neighbouring duchy; the court politics of the small Emilian states. *Modena*

### Liguria (for the night of 4 December)
- ★ **Ilja Leonard Pfeijffer, *Brieven uit Genua*** (2014). NL. A Dutch writer settles in Genoa. *Liguria*

### The Riviera and Menton
- ★ **Katherine Mansfield, letters and journal** (Menton 1920–21, Villa Isola Bella); short stories written there, such as *Miss Brill* and *The Daughters of the Late Colonel*. EN. *Menton*
- ★ **Robert Louis Stevenson, *Ordered South*** (1874). EN. Essay on convalescence on the Riviera after his winter in Menton. *Menton*
- ★ **Colette, *La Naissance du jour*** (1928). FR. Provence and the coast at Saint-Tropez, in her finest prose. *Riviera*
- **F. Scott Fitzgerald, *Tender Is the Night*** (1934). EN. The Riviera of the 1920s. *Riviera*
- **Mary Blume, *Côte d'Azur: Inventing the French Riviera*** (1992). EN. How a poor coast became a myth. *Riviera*
- **Michael Nelson, *Queen Victoria and the Discovery of the Riviera*** (2001). EN. Includes her stay in Menton in 1882. *Menton*
- **Jim Ring, *Riviera: The Rise and Rise of the Côte d'Azur*** (2004). EN. *Riviera*

### Grasse and perfume
- ★ **Patrick Süskind, *Das Parfum*** (1985). DE. The last part of the novel is set in the perfumeries of Grasse. *Grasse*
- ★ **Jean-Claude Ellena, *Journal d'un parfumeur*** (2011). FR. A year in the working life of Hermès' perfumer, in the hills near Grasse. *Grasse*
- **Chandler Burr, *The Perfect Scent*** (2008). EN. Inside the perfume industry, with Ellena as one of its two protagonists. *Grasse*

### Avignon and Provence
- ★ **Petrarch, *Canzoniere*** and the letter on the **ascent of Mont Ventoux** (*Familiares* IV.1, 1336). IT/LA. He first saw Laura in Avignon in 1327; Ventoux is visible from the city. *Avignon*
- ★ **Alphonse Daudet, *Lettres de mon moulin*** (1869). FR. Especially *La Mule du Pape*, set in papal Avignon. *Avignon*
- ★ **Frédéric Mistral, *Mirèio*** (1859) and ***Memòri e raconte*** (1906). OC/FR. The Nobel laureate of the Provençal revival. *Provence*
- **Jean Giono, *Le Hussard sur le toit*** (1951). FR. Upper Provence in the cholera summer of 1832. *Provence*
- ★ **Lawrence Durrell, *Caesar's Vast Ghost: Aspects of Provence*** (1990). EN. His last book: Roman and later Provence, from a long-time resident. *Avignon & Provence*
- ★ **Ford Madox Ford, *Provence: From Minstrels to the Machine*** (1935). EN. Opinionated and wonderful on Provençal life and food. *Provence*
- **Barbara Tuchman, *A Distant Mirror*** (1978). EN. The calamitous fourteenth century, with the Avignon papacy at its centre. *Avignon*
- ★ **Vincent van Gogh, letters** (Arles and Saint-Rémy, 1888–90). NL/FR. The mistral, the light, the olive groves (see §2 for the complete online edition). *Provence*

## 2. Internet sources

### Reference
- **Van Gogh Letters**: the complete annotated edition by the Van Gogh Museum and Huygens Institute, in Dutch/French with English translations. <https://www.vangoghletters.org> ★ *Provence*
- **UNESCO World Heritage List**: the official descriptions for Luxembourg's old quarters and fortifications, Modena's cathedral, tower and Piazza Grande, the historic centre of Avignon and the Pont du Gard. <https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/>
- **UNESCO Intangible Heritage**: "the skills related to perfume in Pays de Grasse" (2018). <https://ich.unesco.org> *Grasse*
- **Michelin Guide** pages for Cheval Blanc, Osteria Francescana and Mirazur. <https://guide.michelin.com>
- **The World's 50 Best**: Mirazur was No. 1 in 2019; Osteria Francescana in 2016 and 2018. Interviews and background. <https://www.theworlds50best.com>

### Museums and sites (for collections, current exhibitions and opening hours)
- **Kunstmuseum Basel**: Holbein, the Amerbach cabinet, the *Praise of Folly* drawings. <https://www.kunstmuseumbasel.ch> *Basel*
- **Fondation Beyeler**. <https://www.fondationbeyeler.ch> *Basel*
- **Vitra Campus and Vitra Design Museum**. <https://www.vitra.com> *Basel*
- **Mudam Luxembourg**. <https://www.mudam.com> *Luxembourg*
- **Musées de Grasse**: the International Perfume Museum and the Villa-Musée Fragonard. <https://www.museesdegrasse.com> *Grasse*
- **Cap Moderne** (Centre des monuments nationaux): E-1027 and the Cabanon. <https://capmoderne.monuments-nationaux.fr> *Menton*
- **Palais des Papes**. <https://www.palais-des-papes.com> *Avignon*
- **Pont du Gard**. <https://www.pontdugard.fr> *Avignon*
- **The Frick Collection**: *Mapping Provenance: Fragonard's Progress of Love*, the story of the panels' journey to Grasse and New York. <https://www.frick.org/blogs/curatorial/mapping_provenance_fragonards_progress_love> *Grasse*

### Architecture
- **Dezeen, Wallpaper\*, ArchDaily**: well-illustrated pieces on the 2021 restoration of E-1027. *Menton*

### Gastronomy and wine
- **Consorzio Tutela Aceto Balsamico Tradizionale di Modena** and **Consorzio del Formaggio Parmigiano Reggiano**: how the two great products of Emilia are made, and visits. *Modena*
- **Carpentras truffle market** (Office de Tourisme): France's best-known black truffle market, Friday mornings from mid-November to March. Friday 11 December falls during our Avignon stay. *Avignon · December*
- **Jancis Robinson** (jancisrobinson.com): reliable background on Lambrusco, Ligurian whites and Châteauneuf-du-Pape. Partly behind a paywall. *Wine*

## 3. Other sources

### Documentaries and television
- **Chef's Table**, season 1, episode 1: Massimo Bottura (Netflix, 2015). EN/IT. *Modena*
- ***Theater of Life*** (Peter Svatek, 2016). Bottura's Refettorio Ambrosiano in Milan during Expo 2015. *Modena*
- ***Pavarotti*** (Ron Howard, 2019). The tenor from Modena. *Modena*
- **Kevin McCloud's *Grand Tour*** (Channel 4, 2009). EN. Following the Grand Tourists through the architecture of France and Italy. *Grand Tour · Architecture*
- **Brian Sewell's *Grand Tour*** (Channel 5, 2006). EN. The acerbic critic retraces the eighteenth-century route through Italy. *Grand Tour*
- **Geert Mak, *In Europa*** (VPRO, 2007–09). NL. The television series of the book. *Grand Tour*
- ***Gotthard*** (SRF/ZDF, 2016). DE. Mini-series about building the Gotthard rail tunnel in the 1870s. *Road*

### Feature films
- ***Ferrari*** (Michael Mann, 2023). Enzo Ferrari in Modena, summer 1957. *Modena*
- ***Perfume: The Story of a Murderer*** (Tom Tykwer, 2006). Süskind's novel, partly set in Grasse. *Grasse*
- ***The Price of Desire*** (Mary McGuckian, 2015). Eileen Gray, Le Corbusier and E-1027. *Menton · Architecture*
- ***Le Hussard sur le toit*** (Jean-Paul Rappeneau, 1995). Giono's Provence. *Provence*
- ***La Gloire de mon père*** and ***Le Château de ma mère*** (Yves Robert, 1990). Pagnol's Provençal childhood. *Provence*
- ***To Catch a Thief*** (Alfred Hitchcock, 1955). The Riviera at its most glamorous, shot on the Corniches. *Riviera*

### Podcasts and radio
- **BBC Radio 4, *In Our Time*: "The Grand Tour"** (30 May 2002). EN. *Grand Tour*
- **BBC Radio 4, *In Our Time*: "Erasmus"** (9 February 2012). EN. *Basel*

### On the way (things to look and listen for)
- **Christmas markets**: Luxembourg (Place d'Armes) and Basel (Barfüsserplatz and Münsterplatz), late November to Christmas. *December*
- **Provençal santons and the thirteen desserts**: the Provençal Christmas traditions that begin in early December. *December · Provence*
- **The Trophy of Augustus at La Turbie**: the Roman monument above Menton on the old Via Julia Augusta. *Road · Architecture*
